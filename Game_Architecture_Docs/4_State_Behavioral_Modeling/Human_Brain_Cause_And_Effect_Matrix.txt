HUMAN BRAIN: CAUSE AND EFFECT MATRIX (PART 2)
=============================================
1. The NPC Brain requires a rigorous, mathematical mapping of Cause (Stimulus) to Effect (Action Aim).
2. The environment is constantly bombarding the NPC with Contextual Stimuli.
3. This document maps exactly how the Appraisal Engine processes these stimuli.
4. 
5. --- STIMULUS: READING A SCANDALOUS NEWSPAPER ---
6. CAUSE: The NPC interacts with the Newspaper Board, and the published story category is "Scandal".
7. CONTEXT CHECK 1: Is the NPC the subject of the photo?
8. If YES (Subject):
9.   - Fear Spike: +100 (Ostracization)
10.  - Social Drop: Set to 0.
11.  - Resulting Emotion: Furious.
12.  - New Aim: "Attack Newspaper Board" or "Flee Home".
13. If NO (Bystander):
14.   - Entertainment Spike: +80.
15.  - Resulting Emotion: Surprised / Happy (Schadenfreude).
16.  - New Aim: "Find Friends to Gossip".
17. 
18. --- STIMULUS: READING A HEROIC NEWSPAPER ---
19. CAUSE: The published story category is "Heroic".
20. CONTEXT CHECK 1: Is the NPC the subject?
21. If YES:
22.   - Social Connection Spike: +100.
23.  - Resulting Emotion: Happy / Proud.
24.  - New Aim: "Strut in Town Square" (Seek visibility).
25. If NO:
26.   - Entertainment Spike: +50.
27.  - Relationship Modifier: +20 towards the Subject of the photo.
28.  - Resulting Emotion: Happy.
29.  - New Aim: "Wander".
30. 
31. --- STIMULUS: SUDDEN CAMERA FLASH ---
32. CAUSE: The Player hits the Shutter button with Flash enabled within 10 meters of the NPC.
33. APPRAISAL ROUTINE:
34.   - Base Fear Spike: +40.
35.   - Neuroticism Check: If Neuroticism > 0.7, Fear Spike = +80.
36.   - Agreeableness Check: If Agreeableness < 0.3, instantly triggers Anger.
37. RESULTING EMOTIONS: Fearful (if Neurotic), Angry (if Disagreeable), Neutral (if highly Resilient).
38. NEW AIM (Fearful): "Flee to nearest cover".
39. NEW AIM (Angry): "Turn and confront Player".
40. 
41. --- STIMULUS: IGNORED BY A FRIEND ---
42. CAUSE: An NPC executes "Approach for Gossip" but the target's Autonomy is too low, causing the target to execute "Flee Crowd" instead.
43. APPRAISAL ROUTINE:
44.   - The approaching NPC's action fails.
45.   - Social Connection Drop: -25 instantly (Rejection).
46.   - Relationship Modifier: -5 towards the target NPC.
47. RESULTING EMOTION: Sad (due to Social drop) or Angry (due to Relationship drop).
48. NEW AIM: "Seek a bench to sit alone".
49. 
50. --- STIMULUS: THE RAIN (ENVIRONMENTAL) ---
51. CAUSE: Global Weather Manager switches to "Rain".
52. APPRAISAL ROUTINE:
53.   - Continuous Drain: Autonomy drains at 2x speed while outdoors.
54.   - Continuous Drain: Entertainment drops to 0 while outdoors.
55. RESULTING EMOTION: Annoyed / Neutral.
56. NEW AIM: "Seek Shelter" (High priority utility).
57. 
58. --- STIMULUS: TARGETED BY A RAGE (🤬) EMOJI ---
59. CAUSE: An NPC is within the blast radius of another NPC's Rage emoji.
60. APPRAISAL ROUTINE:
61.   - Emotional Infection: The target must roll a Resilience check against the caster's Rage.
62.   - Failure: Autonomy drops by -30 instantly.
63. RESULTING EMOTION: Angry or Fearful.
64. NEW AIM: "Argue back" (if Angry) or "Flee" (if Fearful).
65. 
66. --- STIMULUS: APPROACHED BY A PLAYER (HIGH RELATIONSHIP) ---
67. CAUSE: Player walks within 3 meters. Relationship with Player > 50.
68. APPRAISAL ROUTINE:
69.   - Social Connection Spike: +20.
70. RESULTING EMOTION: Happy.
71. NEW AIM: "Stop and wave at Player".
72. 
73. --- STIMULUS: APPROACHED BY A PLAYER (LOW RELATIONSHIP) ---
74. CAUSE: Player walks within 3 meters. Relationship with Player < -50.
75. APPRAISAL ROUTINE:
76.   - Fear Spike: +30.
77. RESULTING EMOTION: Angry / Disgusted.
78. NEW AIM: "Turn back and walk away rapidly".
79. 
80. --- CONTEXT MEMORY STREAM (THE LEDGER) ---
81. Every single Cause and Effect is written to the `NPCMemoryStream`.
82. The ledger stores objects of type `MemoryEvent(Timestamp, StimulusType, SourceID, ResultingEmotion)`.
83. The Brain uses this ledger to answer the question: "Why am I feeling this way?"
84. When the Target Inspector UI queries the Brain, the Brain checks the most recent MemoryEvent.
85. If the most recent event is `(12:00, CameraFlash, Player, Angry)`,
86. The UI will output Context: "Angry because the Player flashed a camera in my face at 12:00."
87. This makes the simulation 100% transparent and perfectly legible to the player.
