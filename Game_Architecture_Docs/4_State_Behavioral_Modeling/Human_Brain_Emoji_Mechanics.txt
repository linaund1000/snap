HUMAN BRAIN: EMOJI MECHANICS AND AOE EFFECTS (PART 4)
=====================================================
1. Emojis in GPOyun are not merely visual feedback icons for the Player.
2. They are physical, systemic events inside the simulation engine.
3. When an NPC emits an emoji, it casts an Area of Effect (AoE) sphere that alters the Brain State of any NPC caught in the blast radius.
4. This creates cascading, butterfly-effect reactions across the town square.
5. 
6. --- THE EMISSION MECHANIC ---
7. Emojis are emitted via the `TriggerReaction(string emoji, Color color)` method.
8. Internally, this method now instantiates an invisible `EmojiCollider` sphere (Radius: 5 meters).
9. Any `NPCBrain` inside this collider processes the emoji as a direct Stimulus.
10. The effect of the emoji depends entirely on the type of emoji cast.
11. 
12. --- THE RAGE EMOJI (🤬) ---
13. Visual: A pulsing red symbol.
14. Trigger: An NPC's Autonomy drops to 0, or they read a scandalous article about themselves.
15. AoE Effect (Tension):
16.   - All NPCs in radius immediately lose -20 Autonomy (Stress).
17.   - If an observing NPC's Autonomy drops below 30 due to this blast, they catch the "Angry" emotion.
18.   - If the observing NPC is highly Agreeable, they instead catch the "Fearful" emotion and gain a "Flee" Aim.
19. Cascade Potential: High. One Rage emoji can stress out a crowd, causing multiple NPCs to hit 0 Autonomy, emitting their own Rage emojis in a chain reaction.
20. 
21. --- THE LOVE EMOJI (🥰) ---
22. Visual: Floating pink hearts.
23. Trigger: Two friends successfully complete a gossip session, or an NPC is photographed by a highly respected Player.
24. AoE Effect (Warmth):
25.   - All NPCs in radius instantly regain +15 Social Connection.
26.   - All NPCs in radius gain a +2 Relationship boost towards the caster.
27.   - If an observing NPC is "Sad", this emoji acts as a cure, immediately returning them to "Neutral".
28. Cascade Potential: Low. Love emojis stabilize the environment and prevent rage cascades.
29. 
30. --- THE HEARTBREAK EMOJI (💔) ---
31. Visual: A cracking terracotta heart.
32. Trigger: An NPC is rejected socially, or reads a scandalous article about a friend.
33. AoE Effect (Empathy / Schadenfreude):
34.   - The effect depends entirely on the observer's Relationship score with the caster.
35.   - If Observer is a Friend (Rel > 50): They gain the "Sad" emotion (Empathy).
36.   - If Observer is a Rival (Rel < -50): They gain the "Happy" emotion (Schadenfreude) and their Entertainment spikes by +30.
37. Cascade Potential: Medium. Can create polarized crowds where half the NPCs are weeping and the other half are laughing.
38. 
39. --- THE STAR EMOJI (⭐) ---
40. Visual: A glowing gold star.
41. Trigger: An NPC reads a Heroic article about themselves.
42. AoE Effect (Awe):
43.   - All NPCs in radius have their current Utility Action temporarily suspended for 3 seconds.
44.   - A forced "LookAt" command is issued, making all nearby NPCs physically turn their heads to stare at the caster.
45.   - This creates a massive photographic opportunity for the Player (a central subject surrounded by admiring onlookers).
46. 
47. --- THE FEAR EMOJI (😨) ---
48. Visual: Shaking blue/white lines.
49. Trigger: A sudden camera flash from a hostile Player, or a loud environmental noise.
50. AoE Effect (Panic):
51.   - All NPCs in radius inherit a +40 Fear Spike.
52.   - This immediately triggers the "Fleeing" Aim for the entire crowd.
53. Cascade Potential: Extreme. A single camera flash can cause a stampede as the panic spreads from NPC to NPC.
54. 
55. --- SYSTEMIC RULES FOR AOE ---
56. - Line of Sight (LoS): Emojis ignore walls. If a Rage emoji happens inside a house, a passing NPC outside will still feel the stress. This simulates hearing the argument through the walls.
57. - Diminishing Returns: If an NPC is hit by the same emoji type 3 times in 10 seconds, the effect is reduced by 50% each time (Desensitization).
58. - Player Immunity: The Player's stats are not affected by emojis, but the Player's HUD will pick up the emotional state if they aim the Viewfinder at the caster.
59. 
60. --- IMPLEMENTATION IN THE C# ARCHITECTURE ---
61. To support this, `HUDManager.SpawnEmojiReaction` must be heavily refactored.
62. It must take a reference to the casting `NPCBrain`.
63. It must execute `Physics.OverlapSphere` to find all nearby `NPCBrain` components.
64. It must call `targetBrain.ProcessEmojiStimulus(EmojiType, CasterID)` on each target.
65. This completely bridges the UI layer with the core behavioral simulation.
