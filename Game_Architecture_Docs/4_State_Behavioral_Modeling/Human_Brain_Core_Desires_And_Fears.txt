HUMAN BRAIN: CORE DESIRES AND EXISTENTIAL FEARS (PART 1)
======================================================
1. The NPC Brain operates fundamentally on a physiological layer of Desires (Aims) and psychological Fears.
2. Unlike a traditional state machine, these desires are continuous floats, evaluated at every simulation tick.
3. The absolute priority of the Utility AI is to fulfill the most critical deficit across these vectors.
4. Desires decay over time, mimicking human entropy. Fears spike instantly based on environmental stimuli.
5. 
6. --- DESIRE 1: SOCIAL CONNECTION (THE HERD INSTINCT) ---
7. Social Connection tracks the innate human need to be perceived and validated by the tribe.
8. Baseline value: 100 (Fully satiated).
9. Decay rate: -0.5 per second (accelerated for Extroverts, decelerated for Introverts).
10. Deficit Threshold (Low): 40. Below 40, the NPC begins feeling "Lonely".
11. Critical Deficit (Critical): 15. Below 15, the NPC experiences deep "Sadness".
12. Aims triggered by deficit: "Seeking Gossip", "Hugging Friends", "Group Hangout".
13. Social Connection is satisfied by physical proximity, active dialogue, and exchanging positive emojis.
14. It is destroyed by witnessing friends argue, being the target of a Rage (🤬) emoji, or isolation.
15. Extroverts have a high baseline ceiling but rapidly decaying Social Connection.
16. Introverts have a low baseline ceiling and slowly decaying Social Connection.
17. 
18. --- DESIRE 2: ENTERTAINMENT AND CURIOSITY ---
19. Entertainment drives the town's simulation cycle. NPCs must consume information to prevent apathy.
20. Baseline value: 100 (Fully satiated).
21. Decay rate: -1.0 per second. The fastest decaying need in the simulation.
22. Deficit Threshold: 50. Below 50, the NPC feels "Bored".
23. Critical Deficit: 20. Below 20, the NPC becomes "Apathetic" and may sit on benches indefinitely.
24. Aims triggered by deficit: "Wandering to Plaza", "Reading Newspaper", "Observing Commotion".
25. Entertainment is exclusively satisfied by the Player's actions (publishing newspapers) or dramatic events.
26. If an NPC reads a scandalous headline, their Entertainment spikes by +80 instantly.
27. If an NPC reads a boring headline (Low Interest), Entertainment only increases by +10.
28. This creates a systemic reliance on the Player to provide drama to keep the town "alive".
29. 
30. --- DESIRE 3: AUTONOMY AND REST (THE INTROVERSION DRIVE) ---
31. Autonomy is the psychological battery. It drains when the NPC is surrounded by others.
32. Baseline value: 100 (Fully Rested).
33. Drain rate: -2.0 per second *per nearby NPC*.
34. Deficit Threshold: 30. Below 30, the NPC feels "Overwhelmed".
35. Critical Deficit: 10. Below 10, the NPC becomes "Angry" or "Furious".
36. Aims triggered by deficit: "Fleeing Crowd", "Walking Home", "Chilling Alone in Forest".
37. Autonomy is restored rapidly (+5.0 per second) when the NPC is completely alone.
38. This guarantees that crowds naturally disperse. An infinite gathering is impossible.
39. Once Autonomy hits 0, the NPC will forcibly drop whatever they are doing, emit a 🤬, and leave.
40. 
41. --- FEAR 1: INTRUSIVE SURVEILLANCE (THE CAMERA THREAT) ---
42. Fear is not a decaying need; it is an immediate survival response to stimuli.
43. The primary fear for all NPCs is the Player's Camera, representing an invasion of privacy.
44. When the Player raises the Viewfinder, a Raycast intersects the NPC's vision cone.
45. This instantly spikes the "Surveillance Fear" metric.
46. If the NPC is Neurotic, the spike is multiplied by 3x.
47. Resulting Emotion: Fearful -> Angry.
48. Aims triggered: "Covering Face", "Fleeing Player", "Confronting Player".
49. The fear spike decays slowly (-5.0 per second). 
50. If the Player takes a flash photo, the fear spikes to maximum (100) instantly.
51. 
52. --- FEAR 2: SOCIAL OSTRACIZATION (THE SCANDAL THREAT) ---
53. The deepest psychological fear is being publicly humiliated in the Newspaper.
54. When an NPC reads the Newspaper, the Brain checks the Primary Subject of the photo.
55. If the NPC is the subject of a "Scandal" or "Disaster" category, Social Ostracization triggers.
56. This is a catastrophic psychological event.
57. Social Connection drops to 0 instantly.
58. Autonomy drops to 0 instantly.
59. Resulting Emotion: Devastation / Fury.
60. Aims triggered: "Running Home", "Weeping", "Attacking the Newspaper Board".
61. The NPC will completely avoid the town square for the next 24 in-game hours.
62. 
63. --- EMOTIONAL MAPPING MATRIX ---
64. Emotions are the *symptom*, not the *disease*.
65. - HAPPY: Social > 60 AND Autonomy > 40.
66. - SAD: Social < 20 AND Fear = 0.
67. - ANGRY: Autonomy < 10 OR Ostracization Triggered.
68. - FEARFUL: Surveillance Fear > 50.
69. - BORED: Entertainment < 40 AND Autonomy > 60.
70. - SURPRISED: Sudden +50 spike in Entertainment (Reading a shocking headline).
71. - DISGUSTED: Proximity to an NPC with <-50 Relationship score.
72. 
73. --- PHYSIOLOGICAL FATIGUE (ENERGY) ---
74. Underneath psychological desires lies pure physiological Energy.
75. Energy strictly limits the NPC's ability to act on their desires.
76. Baseline: 100.
77. Drain: -0.1 per second constantly.
78. If Energy < 20, the NPC cannot Run.
79. If Energy < 5, the NPC will collapse and sleep on the ground, ignoring all other desires.
80. 
81. --- THE ROLE OF PERSONALITY (THE BIG FIVE) ---
82. The NPCPersonalityData ScriptableObject acts as the filter for all Needs.
83. OPENNESS: Modifies Entertainment baseline. High openness = gets bored faster.
84. CONSCIENTIOUSNESS: Modifies Energy drain. High conscientiousness = walks slower, conserves energy.
85. EXTRAVERSION: Modifies Social Connection decay. High extraversion = demands constant interaction.
86. AGREEABLENESS: Modifies Anger threshold. High agreeableness = tolerates low Autonomy longer.
87. NEUROTICISM: Modifies Fear spikes. High neuroticism = massive reactions to the camera.
88. 
89. --- SUMMARY OF THE DESIRE ENGINE ---
90. The Brain is an engine constantly trying to balance 4 fluid tanks (Social, Entertainment, Autonomy, Energy)
91. while dodging immediate psychological spikes (Fear).
92. The Utility AI Action Planner looks at these tanks every second.
93. It scores every possible Action based on which tank is the emptiest.
94. If Entertainment is empty, "ReadNewsAction" scores highest.
95. If Autonomy is empty, "GoHomeAction" scores highest.
96. The current state of these tanks is precisely what will be displayed in the new Target Inspector UI.
