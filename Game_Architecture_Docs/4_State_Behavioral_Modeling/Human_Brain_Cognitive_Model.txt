HUMAN BRAIN COGNITIVE MODEL - DEEP ANALYSIS
===========================================

[1. CORE DESIRES, FEARS, AND EMOTIONS]
- The NPC Brain is governed by deep physiological drives (Desires) and survival instincts (Fears).
- EMOTIONS are the transient, visible reactions to how well these Desires and Fears are being managed.

DESIRES (AIMS)
- Social Connection: The drive to gossip, hug, and form bonds. High deficit leads to Sadness.
- Entertainment: The drive to read news or witness events. High deficit leads to Boredom/Apathy.
- Autonomy: The desire to be left alone (Introversion). High deficit leads to Anger.
- Rest: The drive to replenish energy. High deficit leads to Sluggishness/Sadness.

FEARS
- Intrusive Surveillance: The fear of the Player's camera. Triggers Fleeing or Aggression.
- Social Ostracization: The fear of being written about negatively in the Newspaper (Scandals).
- Physical Danger: Fear triggered by sudden loud noises, flashes, or aggressive NPCs.

[2. THE CAUSE AND EFFECT MATRIX]
The following table outlines how environmental contexts effect the cognitive state.

CONTEXT_STIMULUS          | SHIFTS_DESIRE       | SHIFTS_EMOTION | RESULTING_ACTION_AIM
--------------------------|---------------------|----------------|--------------------------
Reading a Scandal         | Increases Fear      | -> Angry       | Aim: Confront the target
Reading a Heroic Story    | Satisfies Entertain | -> Happy       | Aim: Gossip with friends
Camera Flash in Face      | Spikes Fear         | -> Fearful     | Aim: Flee the Player
Ignored by a Friend       | Drops Social        | -> Sad         | Aim: Seek isolation / cry
High Energy / Boredom     | Spikes Entertain    | -> Surprised   | Aim: Wander to Town Square
Nighttime approaches      | Drops Energy        | -> Neutral     | Aim: Go home and sleep
Targeted by Rage Emoji    | Spikes Fear/Anger   | -> Disgusted   | Aim: Argue back or Flee

[3. UNPREDICTABLE HUMAN NATURE (THE CHAOS FACTOR)]
- Humans are not perfect utility calculators. To simulate true "life", the brain includes a Chaos Multiplier.
- The "Whim" System: Every evaluation cycle, there is a 5% chance an NPC will completely ignore their highest logical Utility Need.
- Example: A highly introverted, tired NPC might suddenly experience a "Manic Whim" and sprint to the center of the town to socialize, overriding all logical parameters.
- Hidden Grudges: A relationship score might be positive (+20), but a hidden boolean ("Holds Grudge") might cause an NPC to randomly sabotage or insult their friend.

[4. EMOJI MECHANICAL EFFECTS (AOE INFLUENCE)]
Emojis are no longer just visual popups. They are physical "Emotional Spells" cast into the world.
When an NPC emits an emoji, it casts a spherical Raycast (AoE) that physically alters the Brain State of any NPC caught in the radius.

EMOJI CAST | MECHANICAL EFFECT ON NEARBY OBSERVERS
-----------|----------------------------------------------------
🥰 (Love)  | Instantly satisfies 10% Social Desire for all nearby NPCs. Grants +2 Relationship to caster.
🤬 (Rage)  | Spreads 'Tension'. Nearby NPCs gain +15 Introversion (Desire to leave) or catch the 'Angry' emotion.
💔 (Heart) | Empathy trigger. Nearby Friends gain 'Sad' emotion; Nearby Rivals gain 'Happy' emotion (Schadenfreude).
⭐ (Star)  | Awe trigger. Nearby NPCs pause their current action for 2 seconds to LookAt the caster.
😨 (Fear)  | Panic cascade. Nearby NPCs inherit the Fear emotion and may also trigger Flee actions.

[5. OBSERVATIONAL JOURNAL INTEGRATION]
- The Journal will act as the "God-Eye" Diagnostic Tool.
- When the Player opens the Journal and selects an NPC, they will see a live readout of:
  Current Aim: "I want to isolate myself."
  Current Emotion: "Sad."
  Context: "Because Bob yelled a 🤬 at me."
  Whim State: "Acting logically."
